# HOME — itch.io page

## Title
HOME

## Short description (tagline)
The snow came early. Something came down with it. Find their fires. Bring them home.

## Description

It's her house. They come as far as the fence and no further. They don't like the light.

The others are still out there in the trees. They move by day and keep their fires at night, because the fire is the one thing that holds them back. So it's in the dark you go looking.

**HOME** is a short survival game about leaving the one safe place you have to bring people back to it. Walk out past the fence after dark. Follow the tracks in the snow. Watch for the glow of a fire between the trees. Get everyone home and the house grows.

- Night is when you work. Days are short; you can wait them out at home.
- Six survivors, scattered far. Each one home makes the house stronger.
- Light scares them off. Sound draws them in. A burning fire is ground they will not cross.
- Bullets don't kill them. A hit slows one down and sends it running. But it'll come back, and it learns.
- Sprinting is loud. Walking is quiet.
- Losses are permanent. They go for the person you're escorting first. If one gets you, the run is over.
- Scavenge the woods: weapons, ammo, a medkit for the hurt, tin cans to pull them off the trail, bear traps, torn maps that mark a camp on your compass, scrap for the workbench.
- The house grows with the people in it: a porch lantern that pushes them back, then a bell that pulls every one of them to the fence while you make a run.
- Bring everyone home and the game tells you your time. It was a race the whole way.

## Controls

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| W A S D | Move |
| Shift | Sprint (loud) |
| Mouse / Left click | Aim / Shoot |
| R | Reload |
| 1 to 6 | Pistol, rifle, shotgun, flare gun, auto rifle, sniper, once you have found them |
| G / V / M | Throw a can / set a bear trap / read a map scrap |
| E | Read the notebook by the door, build at the workbench |
| T | Wait for dark (at home, by day) |
| B | Ring the bell (second house level, from the yard) |
| Esc | Pause and controls. Quit and Restart are on the pause screen |
| Any key | Skip the intro |

Play in the browser, or download the Windows build: unzip and run HOME.exe. Play with sound.

## Theme
HOME is the house. Every loop of the game leaves it and comes back to it: the fence is the line they won't cross, the door is where a survivor stops being a burden and becomes a bonus, and the house itself grows as it fills with people. The intro is her sitting in her chair deciding to go out. The ending is everyone else sitting in it.

## Tools used (AI disclosure)
Made in 72 hours for All Tools Allowed #2. AI tools were used throughout; here is exactly where:

- **Claude Code (Anthropic, Claude Fable 5.1)** — most of the C# code, level layout scripts, the compass, item bar and HUD drawing, and the design document, working from my direction and playtest notes.
- **Tripo** — 3D models: the house cabins, survivors, stalker, trees, rocks, shack, truck, snowmobile, tents, props.
- **Mixamo (Adobe)** — character rigging and animation clips.
- **ElevenLabs** — the voice-over lines in the intro.
- **Unity AI asset generation (GPT Image 1.5 through Unity)** — the item and weapon icons in the bar.
- **Unity 6 (URP)** — engine. Footstep recording from Epidemic Sound. Other sounds synthesized in-engine.

Design, story, voice direction, art direction, playtesting and the calls about what stayed and what got cut were mine.
