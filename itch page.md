# HOME — itch.io page draft

## Title
HOME

## Short description (tagline)
The snow came early. Something came down with it. Find the fires. Bring them home.

## Description

It's her house. They come as far as the fence and no further, and she doesn't know why.

Out in the forest, the people who ran are still alive. By day they hide and their fires go cold. Only after dark do they dare to light them. So it's in the dark you go looking.

**HOME** is a short survival game about going out at night to bring people back. Read the tracks in the snow. Watch for the glow of a fire between the trees. Your lantern is how you see, and how they see you. Get everyone home and the house grows.

- A ten-minute day, most of it night. Night is when you work.
- Six survivors, scattered far. Each one home makes the house stronger: more rounds, faster reloads, a longer reach for your lantern.
- Stalkers wake to light and noise. Sprinting is loud. A shot is louder. A survivor's scream wakes the woods.
- Losses are permanent. Their fire goes out for good.

## Controls

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| W A S D | Move |
| Shift | Sprint (loud) |
| Mouse / Left click | Aim / Shoot |
| R | Reload |
| T | Wait for dark (at home, by day) |
| Esc | Pause and controls. Esc again to quit |
| Any key | Skip the intro |

Windows only. Unzip and run HOME.exe. Play with sound.

## Made with
Unity 6 (URP). Character and prop models generated with Tripo, animated with Mixamo. Voice-over generated with ElevenLabs. Code and level work with Claude Code (Anthropic). Sound effects synthesized in-engine. Made for the All Tools Allowed #2 jam, theme HOME.
